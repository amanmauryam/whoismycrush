What is a mutual crush?

A mutual crush is when you both picked each other. On WhoIsMyCrush, that's the one and only moment anything is ever revealed — and only after a real person confirms it.

How a mutual crush is confirmed

  1. 01 — MATCH

    Campus and roll number line up

    Your entry and their identity have to agree on both. A similar name is never enough on its own.

  2. 02 — VERIFY

    A human reviews the pair

    An admin checks the details before either of you hears anything. This is what stops two people with the same roll number from being fused.

  3. 03 — NOTIFY

    You both get one quiet alert

    No name yet, no order, no "they liked you more". Just: it's mutual.

  4. 04 — REVEAL

    Only if you both choose it

    Identities appear only when both sides press reveal. Either of you can stay anonymous or close the match.

What stays hidden, even after a match

  • Who picked whom first.
  • Whether either of you was a #1, #2 or #3.
  • Who else either of you picked.
  • Any private note made during verification.
