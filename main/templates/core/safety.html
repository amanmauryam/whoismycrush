Safety guidelines

A private game is only fun when everyone feels safe. Here is how we try to keep it that way — and what we ask of you.

What we do

  • Verify every match. A person approves each mutual crush before either side is notified.
  • Keep lists and priorities private. No student can ever see another student's entries or order.
  • Require consent to reveal. Identities appear only when both sides agree, and either side can close a match.
  • Let you block and report. Blocking hides you from each other and retires any related entries silently.
  • Limit abuse. Sensitive actions are rate-limited, and duplicate campus identities are blocked.

What we ask of you

  • Only add people you genuinely know from your campus.
  • Don't use the product to pressure, expose or harass anyone.
  • Don't create more than one campus identity.
  • Report anything that feels wrong — reports are private.

If you feel unsafe

Contact your campus authorities or local emergency services first if you are in immediate danger. Within the product, block the person and file a report; reports are reviewed and repeat misuse ends the account.
