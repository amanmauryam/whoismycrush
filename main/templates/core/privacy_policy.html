Privacy

Your secrets belong to you. Here is exactly what that means.

What stays private, always

  • Your crush list. Nobody can see who you added.
  • Your priorities. The order of your three entries is never shown to a student, ever.
  • Who else you added. There is no public list, feed, or leaderboard.
  • The fact that you added someone at all. No badge, no counter, and we never notify them.
  • Your full roll number. It is used for identity matching inside your campus only. Other students see at most the last three digits.

What we collect

  • Your email, for sign-in and the private "it's mutual" alert.
  • Your campus identity: full name, roll number, course, year, section, department, gender, date of birth.
  • The crush details you type by hand, stored against internal ids and never exposed.

What we never do

  • Tell anyone that they were entered as a crush.
  • Announce a match before a human verifies it.
  • Show who entered first, or in which position.
  • Reveal identity without both sides agreeing.
  • Offer a student search or let anyone browse your campus.
  • Sell or share your data.

Your choices

  • Delete all crush entries at any time.
  • Delete your account and campus ID entirely.
