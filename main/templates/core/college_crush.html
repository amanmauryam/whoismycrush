College crush, kept on campus

A college crush is a small, real world: the same lectures, the same canteen, the same notice board. WhoIsMyCrush keeps the whole game inside that world.

Why campus-only makes a match mean more

A match only ever forms between two students at the same college. That one rule changes everything. There's no stranger from the internet on the other end of a match — it's someone who could be in your next class. It also makes verification sane: a matcher can trust campus and roll number instead of guessing from a name.

One campus identity, verified

  • You build a private campus ID with your roll number.
  • Only one active identity per student per campus.
  • Other students never see your full roll number — at most the last three digits.

Bring your campus in

If your college isn't on the platform yet, you can add it in a few seconds when you sign up. Every other student who searches for it will find it after you. The more of your campus joins, the more a match means.
