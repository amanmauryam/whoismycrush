What is a secret crush?

A secret crush is exactly what it sounds like: someone you like, that you haven't told. The feeling is real — the only missing piece is knowing whether it's mutual.

Why secret crushes stay secret

Most crushes stay quiet for a very sensible reason: saying it out loud is risky. If the answer is no, you can't take it back. So people wait, and often the moment just passes. WhoIsMyCrush exists for that gap — the part where you're not ready to say it, but you'd still love to know.

How your secret crush is handled here

  • You type them in by hand. There is no student search and no public roster. You give the details you already know.
  • They don't need an account. If they're not on the platform yet, your entry waits quietly until they join.
  • They are never told. Adding someone sends no notification, no badge, and no signal of any kind.
  • Your order stays private. Being your first, second or third pick is stored for your own list management and nothing else.

What a secret crush is not

It isn't a follow, a like, or a swipe. Nobody can browse your campus, and nobody can see that you like them. The only thing that ever leaves the vault is a single verified mutual match.
