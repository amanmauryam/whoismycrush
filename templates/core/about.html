About

WhoIsMyCrush is a secret college game, not a dating app.

The idea

Everyone has someone they've been quietly thinking about. Most of the time nothing ever happens — not because they don't feel it, but because nobody finds out. WhoIsMyCrush is one sealed envelope per student. You put up to three names in it, and you find out only if one of those people did the same with you.

Why anonymity

If picking someone were visible, the game would just be humiliation with extra steps. So nothing about your list is ever shown to anyone: not who you picked, not the order, not who else. Priority is stored purely for internal matching analytics and is never rendered, emailed, or returned by any API.

Who it's for

College students aged 18 and over. One campus identity each, verified against a roll number inside that campus, so a match means two real people at the same college — not a stranger on the internet.

What it is not

There is no public feed, no followers, no likes, no comments, no chat, no stories, no swipe cards, no recommendations engine. The simplicity is the product.
