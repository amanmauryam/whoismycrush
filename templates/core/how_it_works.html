Pick. Wait.
Mystery. Mutual.

Six steps, and not one of them involves a swipe.

  1. 01 — PICK

    Add up to 3 people

    Type their name and roll number by hand — there is no student search. You can't add yourself, and entries only ever match inside your own campus.

  2. 02 — SEAL

    We fold the list into an envelope

    Your picks are locked. Not even we can tell you who picked whom first.

  3. 03 — WAIT

    Nothing happens. That's the point.

    Nobody is notified. No badge, no counter, no "X saw your profile". Silence is the feature.

  4. 04 — MUTUAL

    If they added you too

    A real person checks the details before anything is confirmed. Only then do both of you get one notification: it's mutual.

  5. 05 — REVEAL

    You decide together

    Revealing only happens if both sides press reveal. Either of you can walk away and nothing breaks.

  6. 06 — OPTIONAL

    Send something small

    A rose, a note. Optional on purpose. Matching never costs anything.

What this is not

  • Not a swipe app.
  • Not a feed, a follower count, or a like counter.
  • Not a chat. There is no messaging in the MVP.
  • Not a public profile directory. Nobody can browse your campus roster.
